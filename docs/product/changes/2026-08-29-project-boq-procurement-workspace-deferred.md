# Project BOQ Procurement Workspace — Deferred Direction

Status: product direction only; not implemented

Future VOKA may provide a dedicated dashboard module, separate from the Sales Assistant drawing-to-quotation flow, for complete project BOQs. A master BOQ may contain Civil, Concrete, Architectural, Plumbing, Fire Fighting, Mechanical, Electrical, Low Voltage, Procurement and other sections. VOKA would analyze the document and present structured section cards; opening a section would reveal focused section intelligence.

Potential later workflows include section review, supplier and material identification, RFQ generation, selection of existing suppliers, discovery of additional suppliers in the relevant country, direct RFQ email, quotation comparison and procurement workflow.

This note preserves the direction only. It does not approve a parser, data model, supplier-discovery mechanism, email workflow, procurement architecture or roadmap reordering.
