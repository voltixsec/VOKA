# 01. Executive Vision

VOKA is not an ERP.

VOKA is not a CRM.

VOKA is an AI Sales Operating System designed to become the primary operating environment for every sales organization.

The long-term objective is to replace repetitive sales operations with AI-driven workflows while keeping humans in full control of business decisions.
